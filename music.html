01 / Vinyl

From sketch to shrink-wrap

Three LPs for Requiem, each designed and produced by me, from the first sketch to the pressing plant.

LP · 2023

Karas ir Taika

"War and Peace", a new album split into a War side and a Peace side. I was its art department and back office at once: I arranged the photoshoot with photographer T. Terekas, designed the cover, gatefold and centre labels around its contact sheet of film photos, then took it all the way to the Green Lakes Pressing vinyl factory and onto the merch table.

Read more

Behind the artwork sat everything else a record needs: print-ready files, proofs and pressing deadlines with the factory, the budget and a LATGA grant I secured, contracts and 300 hand-numbered copies. I also made sure the audio arrived in the right shape, arranging masters from sound engineer Pavel Daniels to the technical standards of both vinyl cutting and CD replication.

The album also came out on CD, with 500 copies produced in the Netherlands.

I opened a pre-sale on the band's website and arranged a five-concert tour around the release, in Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Alytus and Ukmergė. The vinyl edition is now nearly sold out, and a second pressing is planned for the near future.

LP reissue · 2024 · originally 1998

Niekam nežinomi žmonės

The band's 1998 debut, remastered and pressed on vinyl for the first time as a gift to longtime fans. It had only ever existed on cassette and CD, so I rebuilt the original artwork as a gatefold for the new format and managed the reissue from master to marbled vinyl.

Read more

The original cover was created by the well-known Lithuanian artist Saulius Urbonavičius-Samas, with photos by Claudia Reichardt. My job was to respect it: I kept as much of the design as possible and reworked it to fit a 12-inch gatefold and new centre labels.

I arranged the remastering of the original 1998 recordings with sound engineer Pavel Daniels, then took the record to the Green Lakes Pressing vinyl factory, in black and in turquoise marble. Around it I handled the licences and paperwork a reissue needs, and opened a pre-sale on the band's website.

The launch shows sold out, two concerts back to back. At the release, DJ Mamania of G&G Sindikatas put it simply: "one of the best Lithuanian rock albums".

LP · 2025 · my concept

Senos naujienos

"Old News", the album Requiem never made. Around 2003 the band planned a new record that never happened, and its songs stayed scattered as singles or went unreleased. I collected ten of them into the album they should have been, and built the whole idea around the headlines they made at the time.

Read more

Among them is the hit "Aš nupirksiu tau namą", which helped win the band the title of best rock band two years in a row and stayed in the radio charts for an incredibly long time: 33 weeks on Radiocentras.

The concept and design are mine. I dug up old articles and photos from newspapers and magazines, mostly from the early 2000s, and turned them into a real newspaper called Senos naujienos. Band leader Tomas Krivickas reads it on the cover, and the full paper is printed on the record's inner sleeve.

Some songs were remastered and some freshly recorded, including a new version of Skandalas with the well-known Lithuanian artist ba. (Benas Aleksandravičius), recorded with sound engineer Pavel Daniels at Rockmergė records. Behind it I ran the budget, the recording sessions, the master files and the licences, then took it to Green Lakes Pressing and launched it with concerts in Ukmergė and Vilnius.

02 / Music videos

Video producer

I produced two Requiem music videos: bringing in the crew, locations, partners and volunteers, running the schedule and shoot days, and launching the finished video to press.

Music video · Jan 2025

Apie ką tau papasakot

An international production directed by Mexican filmmaker Roberto Valdes, who had been part of the creative team on the James Bond film Spectre. It was shot in late autumn around Ukmergė and Siesikai castle. The crew started as 5 people and grew to about 100, with the municipality, local schools, the regional museum and even the police helping out.

Music video · 6 July 2026

Stebuklų šalis

"Land of Miracles", a musical gift to Lithuania released on Statehood Day. For the climax scene on Gediminas Hill in Vilnius, 42 municipalities sent representatives with their flags. 70 flags flew at once, including 17 painted by students of the Ukmergė Art School, alongside scouts, youth organisations and volunteers.
