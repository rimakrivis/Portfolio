Approach

Pipeline

  • Cleaning: NLTK normalisation, stop-word removal and Snowball stemming.
  • Features: TF-IDF on 1–2-grams (min_df 5, max_df 0.8) plus FastText word embeddings.
  • Models: Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, Random Forest and SVM, compared on the same validation split.
  • Ensemble: a probability-averaging hybrid of NB + LR + FastText beat every single model.

Random Forest scored 99.97% on training data but only 90.5% on validation, a textbook case of overfitting. Spotting that early is why we went with the lighter, better-generalising ensemble.
