01 / The story

I started in science with a BSc in Biochemistry from Vilnius University. Then I spent 11+ years running cross-functional projects across e-commerce, branding, digital marketing and the music industry: record releases, tours and TV appearances, with 40+ major campaigns delivered since 2017.

Along the way I kept building things myself: websites, shops, campaigns, workflows. In 2026 I completed Ironhack's intensive 400-hour AI Engineering bootcamp to do that properly, with LLMs, agents, RAG and machine learning.

My most recent build, DropOperator, joins both worlds: a production multi-agent platform for exactly the music-release work I used to do by hand. I take full ownership of what I build, and I now bring that decade of delivery into how I ship AI systems.

A fast learner, with proof

I went from no production code to a live multi-agent AI platform with RAG, a FastAPI backend and a Next.js frontend during a 400-hour bootcamp. Before that I learned biochemistry, e-commerce, web design, marketing, artist management and vinyl production on the job. When a role needs a tool I haven't used yet, I learn it the way I learn everything: by building something real with it.
